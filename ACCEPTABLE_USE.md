# Acceptable use

ProfileFleet is intended for authorized browser-session isolation, QA,
accessibility testing, privacy research, internal tools, and automation of
accounts and systems you own or have permission to operate.

Do not use ProfileFleet for:

- credential stuffing, password spraying, or account takeover;
- automated account creation, fake engagement, or platform manipulation;
- bypassing CAPTCHA, access controls, rate limits, or fraud defenses;
- collecting personal data without a lawful basis and appropriate consent;
- evading sanctions, contractual restrictions, or applicable law;
- automating a third-party service without authorization.

Engine adapters and downstream applications remain subject to their own
licenses and service terms. Project maintainers may decline support for use
cases that conflict with this policy.
